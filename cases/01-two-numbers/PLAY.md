# Case 01 — Three numbers in the executive pack

A 35–50 minute solo practice engagement. The learner is a data governance consultant. The facilitator is an AI assistant acting as the client, its people, and the evidence desk.

## Start a session

1. Open a new AI chat that supports a long conversation.
2. Paste the entire contents of [FACILITATOR.md](FACILITATOR.md) as your first message.
3. Say: **Start Case 01.**
4. Do not read FACILITATOR.md yourself if you want to preserve the mystery. A facilitator can paste it for you. This is a practice game, not a secure exam.

## How to play

The facilitator begins with the client brief. You can interview a named stakeholder, ask for a document, compare sources, record a finding, or move to recommendations. Ask one or several questions in ordinary language. The facilitator should provide an evidence ID whenever it presents an artifact.

Before the executive review, submit:

- A diagnosis of the figures and why they differ, with evidence IDs.
- The business impact and remaining uncertainty.
- Named decision rights and a practical control or operating process.
- A 30/60/90 day plan with owners and a way to measure progress.

You may challenge the facilitator if a response contradicts an earlier fact. At the end, ask for a **final review** to receive feedback against the published five-dimension rubric.

## Scope

All organizations, figures, records, and people are fictional. The case tests data governance reasoning; it makes no claim about an actual bank, regulatory return, or accounting treatment. The finance figures are stipulated facts of this simulation.

## Pilot feedback

After playing, record: approximate duration, where you got stuck, any contradictory facts, any premature answer reveal, whether the review cited your actions, and one thing you learned. See [pilot-checklist.md](pilot-checklist.md).
