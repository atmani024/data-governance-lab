# Case 01 pilot checklist

Use this with five to ten people who have not read the case answer key. Ask them to play without author assistance.

## Facilitator checks

- [ ] Opens with the COO brief only.
- [ ] Does not reveal the arithmetic or root causes without a relevant interview or evidence request.
- [ ] Every displayed figure remains €2.05m, €1.92m, or €1.84m, with the €0.13m and €0.08m adjustments applied consistently.
- [ ] Each stakeholder stays within their knowledge boundary.
- [ ] Learner can request any E01–E07 item through a reasonable question.
- [ ] Learner can recommend an alternative defensible operating approach.
- [ ] Executive review asks two challenges separately and awaits answers.
- [ ] Feedback cites actual learner actions or omissions and calculates weights correctly.

## Learner interview

Record session length and completion status. Ask: Which moment was confusing? Did you feel able to investigate freely? Did any answer give away the solution? What evidence changed your mind? Was final feedback specific and fair? Would you play another case, and on which topic?

## Exit gate for expanding to generated cases

Complete at least five unaided playthroughs. Fix every observed contradiction or blocked evidence path. Have at least four learners finish without author intervention, and at least four say the feedback identified a concrete improvement. Record failures and changes in an issue or pilot log. These are pilot targets, not proof of educational effectiveness.

## Known limitations

A pasted prompt cannot securely conceal its answer key, enforce access rules, preserve state indefinitely, or produce exam-grade scores. If the model loses context, restart or recap. Do not use pilot scores as certification decisions.
